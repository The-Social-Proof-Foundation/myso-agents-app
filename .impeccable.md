# MySo Agents design context

Confirmed by the owner on October 24, 2026: match the existing MySocial app’s brand and interface.

## Design Context

### Users
Agent owners and organization teams coordinating paid work, encrypted rooms, and private knowledge on both web and desktop. Primary tasks: authorize bounded agents, find/fulfill work, inspect progress and receipts, and buy knowledge through existing MyData P2P one-time/subscription access.

### Brand Personality
Use the existing MySocial visual identity. Reference implementation: `../mysocial-frontend/app/globals.css`, `app/fonts.ts`, `components/ui/button.tsx`, and `public/logo*.svg`. Do not invent a new logo, palette, or interaction identity. Use clear, nontechnical copy for everyday actions.

### Aesthetic Direction
Carry forward MySocial’s restrained neutral surfaces, subtle borders and chartreuse emphasis. Space Grotesk sets body text and all headings (500–600, slightly tight tracking); Chakra Petch is reserved for the wordmark, eyebrow labels and stat numbers (owner decision, October 26, 2026). Motion stays quiet: hover/press states, short fades and capped list staggers, all disabled under reduced motion. Use its actual local font and logo assets. Support the existing light/dark theme patterns and visible focus treatment. Preserve task context on small screens instead of hiding critical actions.

### Design Principles
1. Match MySocial’s real tokens and assets; avoid a separate visual system.
2. Derive status, prices and receipts from authenticated service data; never seed demo activity.
3. Keep plans, delivery, settlement and verified evidence visibly distinct.
4. Act within approved policy without adding unnecessary payment prompts.
5. Show unknown outcomes and access failures plainly; protect private content in every view.
